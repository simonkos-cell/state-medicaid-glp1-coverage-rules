## Coverage before the PDL listing: the pattern

Maddie Potter's annual SDUD plots show Saxenda claims paid in 2019 to 2021 in NH, VA, KS, RI, DE, and WI, well before the dates in the evidence file, which for those states are the dates a weight management class first appears on a PDL in hand. Minnesota (no claims before 2022), Pennsylvania, Mississippi (none before 2023), Massachusetts, Michigan, and Michigan's 2/1/2022 DUR statement look like true adoptions. Khanna et al. excluded Delaware and Kansas for this reason and treated New Hampshire and Virginia as earlier adopters. For w34949, where treatment is any weight-loss GLP-1 coverage, this means six of the fourteen 2021 to 2024 adopters may have been covering Saxenda throughout the panel, and their event dates mark the arrival of Wegovy on the PDL more than the arrival of coverage. A quarterly SDUD check by product for these six states, against the adoption dates, is the next step before any change to the estimation sample; a Wegovy-or-Zepbound exposure definition would be the natural robustness check.

What this means for the w34949 coding. Seven of the nine states Khanna et al. treat agree with us or differ by one quarter. The substantive disagreements are NH, MN, and KS (and VA by one quarter), and in each the annual SDUD plots side with the earlier date: paid Saxenda or Wegovy claims appear one to three years before the PDL document our file keys on. The evidence file records the earliest PDL listing in hand, which is a lower bound on coverage. For Kansas and New Hampshire, Saxenda claims from 2019 mean these states may belong in an always-covered group rather than in an adopter cohort; for Minnesota the adoption quarter is probably 2022 Q1, not 2023 Q1. These need a quarterly SDUD check by product (Saxenda, Wegovy, Zepbound) for KS, NH, MN, VA, DE before any change to the estimation sample; the annual plots cannot place a start quarter.

Khanna's handling (exclude DE, KS, WI; NC as CV-only) is defensible on their reading of the record; our file has dated documents for DE, KS, WI but, for KS, the documents give a Zepbound date and not a Saxenda or Wegovy start.
