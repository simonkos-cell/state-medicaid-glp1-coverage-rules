# State notes: things that do not fit a column

One entry per state where something needs saying that the evidence file and the events file cannot hold. Dated 2026-10-25 unless noted.

- **California.** No PA; the restriction is a Code I diagnosis edit (chronic weight management) with a BMI ICD-10 code accepted from June 14, 2024. Medi-Cal Rx carved the pharmacy benefit out of managed care in 2022, so one rule applies statewide. Khanna et al. note PAs were suspended for most of 2022 during the PBM transition, which explains Wegovy claims before the 2023 Contract Drugs List.
- **Delaware.** SPA DE-24-0010 (August 1, 2024) covers obesity drugs "with co-morbid conditions with prior authorization"; the comorbidity requirement makes the criteria more restrictive than the label for BMI 30 and above. 23 DE Reg 464 (2019) shows Delaware was writing obesity-drug policy before Wegovy existed, and SDUD shows Saxenda claims from 2019. Khanna et al. excluded Delaware for claims before their 2023 Q1 date.
- **Kansas.** Zepbound preferred from April 1, 2024 (KMAP Bulletin 24071); the same bulletin adds PDL PA to Saxenda and Wegovy, which implies they were payable without PDL PA before April 2024. SDUD shows Saxenda claims from 2019 and about 8,000 Wegovy claims in 2023. The non-preferred BMI threshold of 40 is the highest documented in any state. Kansas's 2026 PA form already lists orforglipron (Foundayo).
- **Massachusetts.** Three regimes: Saxenda and Wegovy at FDA-label criteria from January 2, 2024; both non-covered for adult weight loss from January 1, 2025 (Zepbound continued); Wegovy back from February 17, 2026 at parity with Zepbound with a phentermine step and BMI 35, or 30 to 35 with conditions, or 27 with conditions (Pharmacy Facts 269); end of adult weight-management coverage July 3, 2026. All in `state_coverage_events.csv`. Klebanoff's June 2024 "more restrictive" classification does not match the January 2024 Drug List criteria we read, which restate the label; the difference may come from the recertification rule (5% weight loss) or from a mid-2024 change we have not seen.
- **Michigan.** Coverage from February 1, 2022 with "clinical prior authorization for appropriateness" (DUR Board). Criteria document (MHP Common Formulary PDL PA Criteria) is linked in the RA notes but not in the folder. January 1, 2026 restriction is in the events file.
- **Minnesota.** Coverage with PA from December 2021 (SPA MN-21-0022; DHS criteria page dated December 10, 2021), 15 months before the PDL class was created on March 1, 2023. The December 2021 criteria require a three-month diet trial and, for Wegovy, a failed three-month Saxenda trial, so we code `more_restrictive`; Klebanoff (June 2024) codes "PA at FDA label", which may reflect the criteria after the 2023 PDL change. Both are recorded.
- **Mississippi.** The 7/1/2023 criteria (BMI 30, or 27 to 29 with a comorbidity; age 12) restate the label, so we code `fda_label`; Klebanoff codes "more restrictive". Recorded as a disagreement to resolve against the Klebanoff appendix.
- **Missouri.** Zepbound preferred, Wegovy non-preferred (available with a medical-necessity reason) from the October 15, 2024 SmartPA proposal; PDL effective February 1, 2025. Excluded from the research-letter comparison group.
- **New Hampshire.** Saxenda claims in SDUD from 2019; the September 1, 2022 date is when the Weight Management Agents class was added to the FFS PDL. Khanna et al. date coverage to 2021 Q3 from an NH Healthy Families (Centene) policy with a revision log; the current version of that policy (developed 10.25, effective 01.26) excludes GLP-1s and the older version could not be retrieved. Withdrawal effective January 1, 2026 (state notice October 9, 2025).
- **North Carolina.** Wegovy covered for cardiovascular risk reduction only from the May 24, 2024 PDL revision (Khanna's 2024 Q2 date); weight management agents added August 1, 2024; coverage ended October 1, 2025 and was reinstated December 12, 2025 at the prior criteria. The PA form in the folder is the later cardioprotection, NASH and sleep apnea version.
- **Pennsylvania.** Saxenda and Wegovy preferred with PA and QL from the January 9, 2023 PDL; Zepbound added January 8, 2024; coverage for weight loss ended January 1, 2026 (Medical Assistance Bulletin, November 24, 2025). Criteria document not in the folder; Klebanoff codes PA at FDA label.
- **Rhode Island.** The FFS PDL applies a clinical PA to the whole Weight Management class (review date 10/18/2022). Neighborhood Health Plan of RI, the largest Medicaid MCO, has had a weight-loss drug policy since July 2018 that covers Saxenda and Wegovy with a six-month program requirement and a step through Contrave or Wegovy before Saxenda. RI coverage therefore predates the FFS PDL date; Khanna et al. left RI's criteria undetermined.
- **South Carolina.** No documents in the folder. Coverage November 1, 2024 to January 1, 2026 rests on the evidence file's citation of the November 2024 Single PDL and the SC Daily Gazette.
- **Tennessee.** Wegovy appeared in the diabetes class of the October 1, 2024 PDL; weight-loss coverage from August 1, 2025 (PDL and SPA TN-25-0006). Criteria are in the Optum Rx TennCare criteria document, not in the folder.
- **Utah.** Coverage July 1, 2025 (PA criteria effective date in the PDL resources); the pilot's June 30, 2026 end rests on one news source (open issue).
- **Virginia.** Weight-loss drugs covered under a service-authorization process since at least 2010 (a weight-loss SA criteria form dated 2010 exists in the Wayback), with Saxenda claims from 2019 and Wegovy claims in 2021 and 2022; the January 1, 2023 PDL is the earliest formulary in hand listing Wegovy. Criteria tightened June 23, 2023 (BMI 40, or 35 with two chronic conditions; non-GLP-1 trial). From the 8/9/24 PDL Saxenda is the selected GLP-1 and Wegovy and Zepbound are non-preferred.
- **Wisconsin.** Coverage with PA (attachment F-00163 dated 04/2023) before any quick-reference PDL listed the class, which is why the RA notes read "None". The July 1, 2024 criteria are stricter than the label at BMI 27 to 30 (two risk factors) and require a six-month weight-loss treatment plan. Saxenda claims in SDUD from 2021 Q1. Khanna et al. excluded Wisconsin as unclear.
- **Never-covering states with partial coverage.** Alabama, Hawaii, Louisiana, Washington: Wegovy for cardiovascular risk reduction only. West Virginia: Zepbound for obstructive sleep apnea. Georgia: Saxenda non-preferred with PA for ages 12 to 17 (RA notes), to check before using Georgia as never-treated in an adolescent analysis.

## Criteria documents retrieved online 2026-10-25 (round two)

Eight of the nine states that read `not_documented` after the first pass now have a criteria document:

- **Pennsylvania.** PA Health & Wellness (Centene MCO) Obesity Treatment Agents form (eff. 1/6/2025) requires a concurrent diabetes diagnosis or an antidiabetic drug in the past 120 days before a preferred GLP-1 for obesity, which is more restrictive than the FDA label. This is the MCO form, not the statewide FFS criteria Klebanoff coded as PA-at-label for June 2024, so the Klebanoff column is unchanged and the discrepancy is recorded.
- **North Carolina.** Carolina Complete Health GLP-1 Weight Management form (version 202407): BMI 30, or 27 with a comorbidity; adolescents 95th percentile or 85th with a comorbidity; 5% weight loss to renew; Saxenda and Zepbound non-preferred. Coded fda_label.
- **Kansas.** KDHE AOM criteria (reviewed 8/19/2026; prior 1/17/2024, Khanna's cited source): BMI 30 or 27 with comorbidity for the preferred agents, with a BMI-40 high-cost tier that is waived for Zepbound (4/1/24) and Wegovy (6/1/25). The January 2024 version set initial weight-loss checkpoints (3% Saxenda, 5% Wegovy, 6% Zepbound). Confirms Foundayo (orforglipron) is covered.
- **Michigan.** The 2022 to 2025 era was appropriateness PA only; the MIRx 1/1/2026 criteria require morbid obesity (BMI 40 or higher) and frame GLP-1 use as averting bariatric surgery. Both are recorded; the 2026 criteria match the restriction already in the events file.
- **Utah.** FFS PA form (7/1/25) requires 6 months of dietitian-supervised lifestyle trials before the drug; reclassified from not_documented to more_restrictive.
- **Tennessee.** TennCare Optum Rx provider notice (eff. 8/1/2025): BMI 30 or 27 with comorbidity; 5% to renew; Wegovy and Zepbound preferred, Saxenda non-preferred. Coded fda_label.
- **Rhode Island.** Neighborhood Health Plan of RI policy (reviewed through 5/2024) requires a 6-month supervised weight-management program before drug therapy; reclassified to more_restrictive (MCO-level).
- **Delaware.** Delaware First Health DE.PMN.50 (eff. 01.23, Khanna's cited source): BMI 30 with risk-factor logic and a three-month low-calorie diet trial; confirms the more_restrictive coding inferred from SPA DE-24-0010.

Still not documented: **South Carolina.** SCDHHS does not post a separate GLP-1 criteria document; the restriction lives in the Single PDL, which is not in the folder. Left as `not_documented` with the PDL named as the document to obtain.

## Disagreements with Khanna, Wang and Venkataramani (2026) that are findings, not errors

1. Start dates for NH, VA, KS, RI, DE, WI: their reading (earlier, or unclear) is supported by SDUD claims; ours are PDL listing dates and are lower bounds.
2. North Carolina: their 2024 Q2 is the cardiovascular-only listing; our 2024 Q3 is the weight-management addition.
3. PA stringency for Minnesota (ours more restrictive, theirs label) and Mississippi (ours label, theirs more restrictive), as above.
